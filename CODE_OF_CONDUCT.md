# Community conduct

Treat writers and readers with care. Critique the work, explain what you noticed,
and respect the writer's stated questions. Disagreement about craft is welcome;
harassment, threats, discrimination, impersonation, spam, and sharing another
person's private information are not.

Respect authorship. Share writing you have the right to share, give credit where
appropriate, and keep private drafts and messages private. Use reader notes and
theme labels to help people choose what they read.

These expectations apply to the workshop, circles, messages, issues, and pull
requests. Report workshop concerns to the operator using the report controls or
private feedback. Report repository concerns privately to its maintainers.
Moderators may remove content or restrict participation, consider context, and
handle reports without publishing identifying information.

Help keep this a welcoming place to try, revise, and learn.
